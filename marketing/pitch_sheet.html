How the text-back works two flows, fully automatic, matched to your trade

1 · Pick a service and a time

Appointment trades: braiders, nails, lashes, barbers, stylists, massage
  1. Missed call triggers a friendly text from your business.
  2. Caller gives their name and picks a service from your menu.
  3. They choose a day and one of your open start times.
  4. Time is held while they pay your deposit through Stripe.
→ You get BOOKED + PAID: name, service, time.

2 · Describe the job

Job trades: plumbing, HVAC, electrical, handyman, cleaning, landscaping
  1. Missed call triggers a friendly text from your business.
  2. Caller gives their name and describes the job ("water heater leak").
  3. They pick one of your next open windows, or ask for a callback.
  4. Window is held while they pay your deposit through Stripe.
→ You get BOOKED + PAID: name, job, window. Urgent jobs flagged.
