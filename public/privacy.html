Privacy Policy

Last updated Oct 22, 2026

Note: this document is a plain-English draft and is pending review by legal counsel. It is not legal advice.

This Privacy Policy explains how TryBuzzCall LLC ("we," "us") collects, uses, shares, and protects information through BuzzCall, our missed-call text-back service, and through this website, trybuzzcall.com.

BuzzCall supports Home Services businesses (plumbing and HVAC) and Braiders ("Shops"). A missed call is not SMS consent. SMS is off by default; automatic text-back requires a recorded, active customer-service permission grant for the specific business and sender. Separately permissioned owners may receive alerts. Permitted conversations can collect your name and service need, offer appointments, and provide optional deposit links. In that role we process information on behalf of the Shop you contacted. These draft requirements are not a compliance certification or evidence of carrier approval.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile numbers and SMS opt-in data and consent are not shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except the service providers that help us deliver the messaging service (such as our SMS carrier provider).

1. Information we collect

From callers and customers of Shops

  • Phone number and call details: the number you called from, the Shop number you called, the time of the call, and whether it was answered or missed.
  • Name and service details you give us by text, such as your name, a plumbing/HVAC problem or requested braid style, your address if you share it, urgency where relevant to Home Services, and your preferred appointment time or callback window.
  • Message content: the text messages you send to and receive from the Shop's BuzzCall number. An incoming message does not itself create permission to send application texts.
  • Permission and revocation records: recipient number, configured business identifier and sending number, separate customer-service (customer_service) or owner-alert (owner_alerts) purpose, consent and recording timestamps, source of the record, disclosure version, and a non-secret evidence reference. Records also include revocation/opt-out timestamps and source and delivery-block status as applicable, so an opt-out can be honored. The Shop or authorized operator retains the actual written opt-in evidence in separate secured storage; an application reference is not the evidence itself. Evidence references must not contain passwords, authentication tokens, or URLs.
  • Booking and deposit records: the time slot you picked, booking status, deposit amount, and whether the deposit was paid or refunded.
  • Payment information: deposits are paid on a secure checkout page hosted by Stripe. Your card number and other card details go directly to Stripe. We do not receive or store your full card number or card security code. We receive limited information from Stripe, such as payment status, amount, and a transaction reference.

From Shops (our business customers)

  • Business name, owner or contact name, business phone numbers, email address, business hours and service details, and information needed for carrier (A2P 10DLC) texting registration.
  • Subscription billing details, handled by Stripe (we do not store full card numbers).
  • Emails you submit to our waitlist on this website.

From this website

  • Our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical data, such as IP address, browser type, pages requested, and the date and time of a visit, for security and troubleshooting.
  • We do not currently use advertising cookies or third-party analytics on this website.

2. How we use information

  • To provide nonmarketing customer-service texts only while the recorded permission for that business, sender, and purpose is active, including eligible missed-call follow-ups.
  • To offer appointment times, hold a slot, and, when permission allows, send deposit links and booking confirmations. Payment does not grant SMS permission or override revocation.
  • To make service details and booking status available to the Shop so it can serve you. Owner SMS alerts require their own active owner-alert permission.
  • To process deposits and refunds through Stripe.
  • To check permission before every application SMS, honor keyword and natural-language opt-outs, and handle help requests without duplicating provider replies.
  • To provide, maintain, secure, and improve BuzzCall, including preventing fraud, abuse, and spam.
  • To bill Shops and provide support. Service and account SMS, including billing reminders, require the separate owner-alert permission.
  • To comply with law, carrier rules, and valid legal requests.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use callers' information to send marketing messages.

3. How we share information

We share information only as described here:

  • With the Shop you contacted. The Shop receives your name, phone number, job details, messages, and booking and deposit status so it can serve you. The Shop's own use of that information is governed by the Shop's own privacy practices.
  • With service providers that process information on our behalf, under contracts or terms that limit how they may use it:
    • Twilio, to send and receive text messages and route calls.
    • Stripe, to process deposits, refunds, and Shop subscription payments.
    • OpenAI (optional, only for Shops that turn it on), to help write natural replies in the text conversation. When enabled, message text and job details from the conversation may be sent to OpenAI to generate a reply.
    • Our website and server hosting provider.
  • For legal reasons, if required by law, subpoena, or other legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of you, a Shop, TryBuzzCall, or others.
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this policy. This does not transfer SMS consent to another business or sender; new permission must be reviewed and recorded before that business sends texts.

As stated above, mobile numbers and SMS opt-in data and consent are never shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

4. Text messages and opt-out

BuzzCall requires recorded written permission before application texts. Missed calls, inbound messages, START, payments, waitlist signup, and merely providing a number do not automatically grant permission. Customer-service and owner-alert permissions are separate and not transferred between businesses or senders. We do not send an unsolicited SMS to obtain initial opt-in. See How text consent works and our SMS Terms. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase; you may call the business instead.

Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT, REVOKE, OPTOUT, and natural-language requests such as "please stop texting me" revoke all purposes locally, including future confirmations and owner/billing alerts. Twilio may send a provider-managed response; an OptOutType START, STOP, or HELP notification does not cause a duplicate application reply. Its optional Advanced Opt-Out is disabled by default and differs from standard keyword filtering.

START only clears a delivery block; it never restores grants. After opt-out, fresh written evidence and a fresh operator-recorded grant are required. If Twilio has blocked delivery, the recipient must first explicitly opt back in with the provider. For help without receiving SMS, contact the Shop or hello@trybuzzcall.com. See the Twilio Messaging Policy and Advanced Opt-Out documentation for provider requirements; these are not approval of this program.

5. How long we keep information

  • Caller, message, booking, and permission records are stored in the application's protected data directory (DATA_DIR) and associated logs. The current implementation has no automatic retention-based deletion or de-identification schedule; ending an account does not automatically erase these records. Operators must review retention and handle deletion requests manually, including logs and any backups, subject to applicable obligations.
  • Consent evidence and revocation history are retained to substantiate permission and keep honoring opt-outs. Actual written evidence is held separately in secured storage; the application retains references, purposes, sources, disclosure versions, and timestamps. The appropriate retention period and access controls must be reviewed with the Shop and counsel; no fixed automatic deletion deadline is promised. Deletion must not silently remove suppression records and cause messaging to resume.
  • Payment and billing records are kept as long as needed for tax, accounting, and chargeback purposes. Stripe keeps its own records under its own policies.
  • Waitlist emails are kept until you ask us to remove them or we stop the waitlist.

6. Security

The application uses authenticated admin tools for permission records. Operators must use HTTPS for live service, restrict access to DATA_DIR, logs, backups, and external written evidence, and keep credentials out of evidence references. On Render, DATA_DIR must be on a persistent disk; operators must verify that consent and revocation records survive restarts. A persistent disk is not by itself an access-control or encryption guarantee. These are deployment responsibilities, not a claim that every installation has been audited. Payment card details are handled by Stripe, not stored by us. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Stop texts: reply STOP to any BuzzCall message.
  • Access, correct, or delete: you can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. Because we process caller information on behalf of Shops, we may work with the Shop you contacted to respond. We may keep information where we must by law, to complete a booking or refund, or to honor an opt-out.
  • State privacy rights: depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state law, such as to know, access, correct, or delete personal information, and to not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We will verify your request, for example by asking you to confirm from the phone number involved, and respond within 45 days.

8. Children

BuzzCall and this website are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.
