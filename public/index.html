How it works

  1. Customer calls, you can't pick up.
  2. They get a friendly text from your business within seconds, asking their name and what's wrong.
  3. They pick one of your next 3 open times and pay a $49 deposit through Stripe, credited to their bill.
  4. You get a text like this (illustration): "✅ BOOKED + PAID $49: [Caller name], [job], [time]. Tap to call." Urgent jobs are flagged.
