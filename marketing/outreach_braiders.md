# BuzzCall for Braiders: Outreach Scripts (Atlanta pilot)

> **Placeholders:** `{first}` stylist's first name · `{studio}` studio/business name · `{you}` your name · `[phone]` · email hello@trybuzzcall.com.
> **Rules:** plain words, short. No made-up numbers, results, client names, or testimonials. Prices are estimates: free 14-day pilot, then $49/mo + ~$5 per paid booking (estimate). Texts to clients come from a local BuzzCall number, not the stylist's own number. The $50 deposit is paid by the client and goes toward their service; it is not a fee to the stylist.
> **Instagram:** send DMs by hand, one at a time, only to business accounts that list booking by phone or DM. If they say no or don't answer after one follow-up, stop.

---

## Instagram DM 1: the missed call

> Hi {first}! Love your work. Quick question: when you're in the middle of a knotless set and a new client calls, what happens to that call? I'm {you} with BuzzCall here in Atlanta. It texts that caller back for you, lets them pick a style and a time, and takes a $50 deposit, all while you keep braiding. Free for 14 days if you want to try it. Want me to send you a 1-page sheet?

## Instagram DM 2: deposits and no-shows

> Hey {first}, I'm {you}, local here in Atlanta. I set up a simple text system for braiders: when you miss a call, the client gets a text, picks their style and day, and pays a $50 deposit to hold the time. The deposit goes toward their service. You just get a text that says "BOOKED + PAID." Free 14-day pilot, nothing to install. Open to a quick look?

## Instagram DM 3: already on Booksy / StyleSeat / GlossGenius

> Hi {first}! I see you book online. That's great for people who go to your link, but some clients still just call. I'm {you} with BuzzCall. When you can't pick up, it texts them back so they don't move on, and it can send them your booking link or book them by text with a deposit. Free to try for 14 days, then $49/mo + about $5 per paid booking (estimate). Want me to show you how it looks?

**Follow-up (once, 3-4 days later):**

> Hi {first}, just checking back. Happy to set up the free 14-day pilot for {studio} and walk you through it in 10 minutes. If it's not for you, no worries at all.

## In person: beauty supply store

*Ask the manager or owner first. Go at a slow time (weekday morning), keep it under 2 minutes, and ask before leaving anything.*

> **To the manager:** "Hi, I'm {you}. I'm local, and I'm starting a service for hair braiders in Atlanta. A lot of braiders shop here. Would it be OK if I left a few one-page flyers by the register or on your board? It's nothing you sell, just a tool for stylists. I'm happy to leave my number in case you have questions."
>
> **If a braider is there and open to talking:** "Do you braid? Quick question: when you're in the middle of someone's hair and your phone rings, do you usually catch that call? I set up something that texts the caller back for you, has them pick their style and a time, and takes a $50 deposit toward the service. The texts come from a local BuzzCall number, so your personal number stays out of it. It's free for 14 days. Can I give you this sheet, or text you the details?"
>
> **If they ask about price:** "Free for 14 days. After that it's about $49 a month plus around $5 when a client actually books and pays a deposit. Those are estimates, and I'll confirm before you pay anything."
>
> **Close:** "Thanks for your time. My number and email are on the sheet: [phone], hello@trybuzzcall.com."

## Objection: "I already have my own clients"

**Say this:**
"That's exactly who this is for. I'm not selling you new clients. It's the people already calling you, the ones you miss mid-braid. They get a text back in seconds and book with a deposit before they call the next stylist. Your clients, just fewer lost ones and fewer no-shows."

**Then ask (let them check their own phone):**
"How many calls did you miss last week? Open your call log. If even one was a new client who booked somewhere else, that's a full braid lost."

**Back it up:**
- The deposit protects the time you block, even for regulars, so fewer no-shows.
- Free 14-day trial, and setup is waived for our first 10 founding shops. If it saves you nothing, you pay nothing.

**Short DM version:**
"Totally get it, you're busy because your clients love you. BuzzCall isn't new clients. It texts back the ones you miss while your hands are in someone's hair, and books them with a deposit. Want to try it free for 14 days?"

**Don't promise:** new client leads or referrals. That isn't built yet.
