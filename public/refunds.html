Customer Deposit Refund Policy

Last updated Nov 11, 2026

For a refund of a booking deposit, contact the shop you booked. The shop is the seller. BuzzCall does not send that refund.

This page explains booking deposits paid by text through BuzzCall. It covers customers of plumbing and HVAC shops, braiders, and other local businesses that use BuzzCall. It also tells the shop what it is responsible for. The shop's own written policy is the policy that applies to your appointment.

If you paid a deposit

You paid the shop, not BuzzCall. The charge is a direct charge on the shop's own Stripe account. The shop is the seller and the merchant of record. BuzzCall is the software that texted you and sent the payment link.

BuzzCall does not hold, escrow, or transfer your money. BuzzCall does not decide whether you get a refund.

The shop sets the deposit amount. You see that amount in the text before you pay. In this pilot, a deposit is at most $50. The text says the deposit goes toward your bill or your service. It is not a fee paid to BuzzCall.

The shop sets the cancellation and refund rules, and the shop should tell you those rules before you pay. Ask the shop what happens if you cancel, if you are not there, or if you decline the work when they arrive.

How to ask for a refund

Contact the shop. Reply to the shop's text, or call the shop at the number you called. The shop refunds you in its own Stripe Dashboard, usually back to the same card. After the shop sends the refund, it often shows on your statement in 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

If you cannot reach the shop, contact BuzzCall support at hello@trybuzzcall.com or (470) 482-8377. We will pass your message to the shop. We will not decide the refund, and we will not send the money.

If the shop does not show up

You may get a text that asks, "Did [Shop] show up for your appointment? Reply YES or NO." Please answer honestly. Your reply is a record for the shop. It is not a promise by BuzzCall to pause a payout or refund you. For a refund, contact the shop.

If you are the shop

You are the merchant of record for a customer deposit. You set and disclose your own deposit and cancellation policy before the customer pays. You refund the customer in your own Stripe Dashboard. You handle disputes and chargebacks on that charge. BuzzCall does not hold, escrow, or transfer the customer's money, does not decide the refund, and does not absorb the dispute. The full terms are in Terms, Section 5.

BuzzCall subscription fees (for businesses)

This page is about deposits your customers pay you. Your own BuzzCall plan is separate, and it is billed by BuzzCall through Stripe. The monthly plan is $49 after a 14-day free trial. The trial converts to paid unless you cancel first, and the subscription renews on its own until you cancel. You can cancel any time and keep access through the period you already paid for. There are no partial refunds of a monthly fee, and no 30-day money-back guarantee on top of the trial. The $99 setup fee is not billed during the trial. Once it is invoiced, it is not refundable. It is waived for the first 10 founding shops as a promotional price reduction. See Terms, Sections 2–4.

Questions

Customers: contact the shop by replying to its text, or call the shop. If you cannot reach the shop, BuzzCall support (TryBuzzCall LLC) is at hello@trybuzzcall.com or (470) 482-8377. We pass the message on. The shop sends any refund.

Shops: the same email and phone, or the billing portal linked from your account.
