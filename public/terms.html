1. The service

BuzzCall is for businesses only, not for personal or household use. When a call to your business goes unanswered, BuzzCall texts the caller back from a number set up for your business, collects the caller's name and problem, offers up to 3 of your open appointment times, can request a booking deposit (default $49) through Stripe that is credited to the customer's bill, and sends you an alert by text. We handle setup and carrier (A2P 10DLC) texting registration with information you provide. Features may change over time.

2. Free pilot and free trial

Pilot. We may offer a free 14-day pilot (currently for the first 10 Georgia shops) that we set up with you without a payment method. Pilots are free of BuzzCall subscription, setup, and per-paid-lead fees, are provided as-is for evaluation, and may be limited or ended at our discretion. A pilot set up without a payment method does not turn into a paid subscription unless you choose a paid plan.

Free trial at signup. If you sign up online, you choose a monthly or annual plan and enter a card. Your plan starts with a free trial (currently 14 days, as shown at checkout). The trial converts to a paid subscription automatically and your card is charged for the first billing period when the trial ends, unless you cancel before the trial ends. We will remind you by text before the trial ends (currently about 3 days before). If no valid payment method is on file when the trial ends, the subscription is cancelled. Setup fee. A one-time setup fee (currently estimated at $99) applies to monthly plans only. It is waived for our first 10 founding shops and on annual plans. It is not charged at signup: it is added to your first invoice after your free trial ends, and if you cancel during the trial you pay no setup fee. Whether it applies to you is shown at checkout.

Carrier registration fees, if any, will be disclosed to you before they are charged. Customer deposits collected during a pilot are handled as described in Section 5.

No fee applies to calls, texts, callback requests, unpaid or expired holds, or bookings without a paid deposit. During a free trial, a plan change takes effect without a charge until the trial ends.
