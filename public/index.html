Do I have to change my business number?

You can keep the number customers call, subject to your carrier's forwarding options. We confirm forwarding and the outbound texting number during onboarding; they aren't necessarily the same number.

What does my customer need to download?

Nothing. The conversation happens over regular text messages, and the deposit link opens Stripe's checkout page. You receive booking alerts by text, too.

Is the customer deposit a BuzzCall fee?

No. A customer deposit is credited toward their service bill. It is separate from the business's subscription and any paid-booking fee. The Home Services example uses $49; braider deposit amounts and terms are agreed separately. Payment routing and payout timing depend on your Stripe setup.

What if the customer cancels?

For Home Services, the deposit is fully refunded if the shop cancels, can't make the slot, or the customer cancels at least 24 hours ahead. If work is declined on site, it may cover the trip fee. Read the Home Services refund policy. Braider deposit and cancellation terms must be agreed before that pilot is activated.

Who is the pilot for?

Georgia owner-operated plumbing and HVAC businesses, plus independent braiders and qualifying loc studios. The best fit has missed new-customer phone inquiries, capacity for more work, and no effective existing call recovery. Other beauty services and industries aren't part of this initial offer.

Does it sync with my booking app or team calendar?

Not currently. The pilot uses configured hours and bookings recorded in BuzzCall, not a live sync with Booksy, StyleSeat or another calendar. A braider's existing booking link can be included in texts. We need to agree how availability stays accurate; multi-truck dispatch and staff scheduling aren't included.

How soon can I go live?

After the required consent process, carrier registration, number setup, availability and payment terms are confirmed. Approval times vary, so we don't promise instant activation. Joining the list is not a subscription or permission to start texting your customers.

What about SMS consent and opting out?

A missed call is not SMS consent. BuzzCall texts only recipients whose written permission is recorded for that business and sending number. Owners need separate permission for alerts. STOP revokes permission; START does not restore it. Frequency varies and message and data rates may apply. Contact us by phone or email for help if texts are blocked. See the Home Services and Braiders texting information; disclosures and carrier approval must be reviewed before activation.
