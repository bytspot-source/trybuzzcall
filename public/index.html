Do I have to change my business number?

No. Keep your current number. We help configure missed-call forwarding so BuzzCall can handle calls you can't answer. We'll confirm the right setup for your carrier during onboarding.

What does my customer need to download?

Nothing. The conversation happens over regular text messages, and the deposit link opens Stripe's checkout page. You receive booking alerts by text, too.

Where does the $49 deposit go?

Your customer pays through Stripe to confirm the booking. That $49 is credited toward their final job bill. It's separate from your shop's base plan and paid-lead fee. Payment routing and payout timing depend on your Stripe setup.

What if the customer cancels?

The deposit is fully refunded if your shop cancels, can't make the slot, or the customer cancels at least 24 hours ahead. If work is declined on site, it may cover the trip fee. See the full refund policy for details.

Who is the pilot for?

Georgia plumbing and HVAC shops running 1–5 trucks. The first 10 founding shops get a free 14-day pilot and waived setup. Join the list and we'll follow up about your shop and availability.

Can callers opt out of texts?

Yes. Callers can reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. Read how texting and consent work.
