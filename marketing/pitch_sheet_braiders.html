How it works 4 steps, all by text

Missed call → text

2

They pick from your menu (knotless, box braids, retwist, twists) and can add size and length.

Why it fits a braiding business

  • Deposit up front: $50 down, credited toward the service.
  • Times that fit the style: a 6-hour knotless set only gets offered where 6 hours are open.
  • No double-booking: held and booked times aren't offered again.
  • Your menu, your hours: set your styles, how long each takes, your days (Saturdays too).
  • They can still talk to you: clients can reply "call" and you get a callback note.
  • A local person to call: we set it up with you, here in Atlanta.
