# BuzzCall: Outreach Kit (TryBuzzCall)

> Builds on `docs/OUTREACH_SMS.md`. Same voice: short, plain, neighborly.
>
> **Positioning (updated):** Text-back alone is a commodity. We sell **booked, paid jobs, not texts.** Lead line: *"$49 deposit in your account before you're off the ladder."* Every message should land on one of these:
> 1. **Booked + paid:** the caller picks a slot and pays a $49 deposit (credited to their bill). The owner gets a "BOOKED + PAID" alert, plus a **weekly recap** of calls caught, jobs booked, and deposits collected.
> 2. **Done for you, no software switch:** keep your current number, everything runs over plain text, no app, no dashboard. We do the setup *and* the carrier texting registration (A2P 10DLC).
> 3. **Local, in person:** a real Georgia person to call, plus ready-made emergency scripts for AC out in a heat wave and frozen/burst pipes in a freeze.
>
> **Who to target (internal only):** Georgia plumbing + HVAC shops with **1–5 trucks** that run on phone, paper, or a calendar, not a field-service software platform. Ask "How do you schedule jobs right now?" If they're already on a full field-service platform, they likely aren't a fit. **Never name competitors to prospects.**
>
> **Placeholders:** `{first}` owner first name · `{shop}` business name · `{city}` their town · `{you}` your name · `[Your phone]` · `hello@trybuzzcall.com` · `[booking link]` · `[refund policy]`.
> **Proof placeholders** stay in brackets until we have real numbers. Do **not** fill with guesses: `[X calls caught]`, `[X jobs booked]`, `[$X deposits collected]`, `[X pilot shops]`, `[testimonial: name, shop, city]`.
>
> **Compliance:** Every text carries opt-out wording ("Reply STOP to opt out"). Only text numbers you have a lawful basis to text (TCPA, A2P 10DLC). For pure cold outreach, calling, walking in, or emailing first is safer than texting. Honor STOP right away and log it.
>
> **Pricing language (estimates, always "starting at"):** free 14-day pilot, no card needed to start · base plan starting at $49/mo · plus a per-paid-lead fee (estimate: $12) charged **only when a caller books and pays the deposit** · setup starting at $99, **waived on an annual plan**. Positioning line: **"You only pay more when you get paid jobs."** Don't mix up the two $49s: the $49/mo base plan is billed to the shop; the $49 deposit is paid by *their customer* through Stripe and **credited to the customer's bill**. It's not a fee to the shop. Refunds: `[refund policy]`.

Imagine this text going to your missed callers, except it books the job and collects a $49 deposit before you're off the ladder. Same number, no app, I set it all up. Free 14-day pilot. ## 5. Phone / walk-in script (about 60 seconds)

**Opener**
> "Hey, is this {first}? I'm {you} with TryBuzzCall. We're local, here in Georgia. Got 30 seconds? I'll be quick."

**Qualify**
> "How many trucks are you running? And how do you schedule jobs right now? Phone and a calendar, or a software system?"
> *(Best fit: 1–5 trucks, phone/paper/calendar.)*

**Problem**
> "When you're on a job and the phone rings, what happens to that call?"
> *(Let them answer. Usually it's "voicemail" or "I call back later.")*
> "Right. And some of those folks won't wait. They call the next guy."

**What it does**
> "So every missed call gets a text back in seconds. It asks their name and what's wrong, offers your next three open times, and takes a $49 deposit that goes on their bill. Then you get a text: 'BOOKED + PAID,' with their name, the job, the time, and a button to call them. Emergencies get flagged. Every week you get a recap: calls caught, jobs booked, deposits collected. So it's not just texts, it's booked, paid jobs."

**No switch, done for you**
> "You keep your same number. No app, no new software. I set it all up, including the carrier registration for business texting. And we've got ready-made scripts for AC going out in a heat wave and pipes bursting in a freeze."

**Offer**
> "First 14 days are free. After that it starts at $49 a month, plus about $12 for each lead that books and pays a deposit. So you only pay more when you get paid jobs. Setup starts at $99, and I waive it if you go annual."

**Close**
> "Want me to set it up this week? I just need your cell and your usual hours."

**If it's a walk-in:** leave the pitch sheet with [Your phone] and hello@trybuzzcall.com filled in. Write the owner's name on top if you got it.

## 7. Emergency scripts (customer-facing text-back wording)

*Seasonal versions of the first text a missed caller gets. Turn them on for heat waves and hard freezes. Adjust the wording to sound like the shop. Keep the STOP line.*

**Heat wave: AC out**
> Sorry we missed your call! This is {shop}. No AC in this heat? Reply with your name + address and we'll get you one of our next open slots. Anyone elderly, a baby, or sick at home? Reply URGENT and we'll call you first. Reply STOP to opt out.

**Freeze: frozen or burst pipe**
> Sorry we missed your call! This is {shop}. Pipe burst or frozen? If water is leaking, shut off your main water valve now if you can. Then reply with your name + address and we'll get you the next open slot. Reply STOP to opt out.

## 9. Objection replies

*Works by text or in person. Add "Reply STOP to opt out" when you send by SMS and it's the first text in a while.*

### "That's too expensive."
> "Totally fair. Here's how I'd look at it: the base plan starts at $49 a month, and the only other charge, about $12, comes when a caller actually books and pays a deposit. So you only pay more when you get paid jobs, and every one of those comes with a $49 deposit already paid. That's why the first 14 days are free. Your weekly recap shows exactly how many jobs it booked and how much it collected before you spend a dime. And if you go annual, I waive the setup."

*Short SMS version:*
> Fair! Base starts at $49/mo, and the ~$12 lead fee only hits when a caller books + pays. And the first 14 days are free. Your weekly recap shows jobs booked + deposits collected before you pay anything. Reply STOP to opt out.

### "I don't want to switch software / learn a new system."
> "You don't have to. Nothing to switch. You keep your same number, it all runs over regular text messages, and there's no app or dashboard. I set it up for you, including the carrier registration for business texting. You just get a text when a job's booked and paid."

*Short SMS version:*
> No switch needed. Same number, all by text, no app. I do the setup + carrier registration for you. Reply STOP to opt out.

### "Won't a deposit scare customers off?"
> "It's $49 and it's credited to their bill, so it's not an extra charge, it's part of what they'd pay anyway. It holds their time slot so they know you're really coming. Refunds follow a clear policy: [refund policy]. And anyone who'd rather talk first can reply 'callback,' and you get the alert to call them."

*Short SMS version:*
> The $49 goes toward their bill, not on top of it, and it holds their slot. Clear refund policy: [refund policy]. Reply STOP to opt out.

### "Where does the deposit money go? Is it a fee?"
> "Your customer pays it through Stripe and it's credited to their bill. It's not a fee to you."

### "I answer my own calls."
> "That's great, and honestly that's why folks trust you. This only kicks in for the ones you *can't* get to: when you're on a job, driving, with family, or it's 2am. It's a backup so those callers get booked instead of moving on. You can always call them yourself."

*Short SMS version:*
> Love that. It only steps in when you can't pick up (on a job, driving, after hours), and it books + collects a deposit so they don't go elsewhere. Reply STOP to opt out.

### "Customers hate bots."
> "I hear you. Nobody likes a phone tree. This is a couple of short, plain texts from your own number: 'Sorry we missed you, what's your name and what's going on?' We'll set up the wording together so it sounds like you. Anyone who'd rather talk to a person can just reply 'callback,' and you get the alert to call them. Plenty of folks would rather text than sit on hold."

*Short SMS version:*
> Get it. It's a couple of friendly texts from your number, worded how you talk, and anyone can reply "callback" to get you instead. Want to see a sample? Reply STOP to opt out.

### "Texting setup sounds like a hassle."
> "That's on me. Business texting needs carrier registration now (it's called A2P 10DLC), and I handle all of it. I'm local, so if anything's off, you call me, a real person in Georgia."
