1. The service

BuzzCall is software for local shops. It is built first for plumbing and HVAC, and also for braiders. It is for businesses, not for personal or household use.

When a call to your shop goes unanswered, BuzzCall texts that caller back on your behalf, asks what they need, offers open appointment times, and can send a link so the customer can pay you a deposit. It also texts you, the owner, about the missed call and any booking. We help with setup and with carrier (A2P 10DLC) texting registration, using information you give us. Features can change over time.

Texts a customer receives are sent on behalf of your shop. Your shop is the sender of those texts. BuzzCall provides the software and the number the text is sent from. See the SMS Terms.
