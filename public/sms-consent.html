How text consent works

Last updated Oct 19, 2026

Note: this document is a plain-English draft and is pending review by legal counsel. It is not legal advice.

BuzzCall Missed-Call Text-Back is operated by TryBuzzCall for local plumbing and HVAC businesses. This page explains how people opt in to BuzzCall text messages, what they receive, and how to opt out.

Opt-in: the customer starts the conversation

BuzzCall only texts people who first contacted the business. We never text purchased lists, and we don't send marketing messages.

1. The customer calls the business.
A customer calls a participating shop's phone number to ask for service.
2. The call is missed.
If no one answers, the caller hears a short message such as "Sorry we missed you. We just sent you a text and will call you back shortly."
3. A conversational text reply.
Within seconds, the caller gets a text from the business's number. The first message names the business and includes STOP and HELP instructions.
4. The customer chooses.
The customer can reply to book, ask for a callback, or reply STOP to end messages. No reply means no further follow-up texts.

Customers can also opt in by texting the business's number directly. Shop owners and staff opt in to booking alerts by giving us their own mobile number during setup.
